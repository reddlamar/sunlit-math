Privacy Policy for Sunlit Math

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Sunlit Math ("the app") is a math practice game for kids. This page explains what information the app handles and how.

What the app stores

When you finish a round, the app saves your score to a local top-10 leaderboard. You choose a cartoon animal to play as (such as Fox or Owl); nobody types a name or any other personal information. The app stores:

  • The animal you chose and its name (for example, "Fox"). Scores saved by earlier versions of the app may instead have a name that was typed in.
  • Your score, which operation you played (addition, subtraction, multiplication, or division), and the difficulty level
  • The date and time you played
This information is stored only on your device, using standard on-device app storage. It is never uploaded, transmitted over the internet, or sent to any server — including to us. We do not have access to it, and it does not leave your device unless you delete the app or clear its data.

What the app does not do

  • No account creation, login, or sign-in of any kind
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs that collect data
  • No location data
  • No access to contacts, photos, camera, or microphone
  • No data is shared with, or sold to, any third party — there is nothing to share, since nothing leaves the device

Children's privacy

Sunlit Math is designed for children. Because all data described above stays on the device and is never collected, transmitted, or viewed by us or anyone else, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else. If you have questions about how this applies to your family, please contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

If this app starts handling data differently in the future (for example, adding an online leaderboard), this page will be updated first, with the date above reflecting the change.
